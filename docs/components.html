Interaction review checklist

Visual priorities, commands and non-game interactions

Space identifiers

1 · Destination
Name stays legible at all times. A gold frame means inspected; the ship markers show actual occupants.
2 · Decision value
Vacant deed: purchase price. Owned deed: current rent. Fee: amount owed. District color is a stripe, not another line of microtext.
3 · Ownership and presence
Flag with seat number: owner. Numbered discs: visiting merchants. Use a separate line when both occur.
4 · Detail on demand
Inspector and accessible label carry district, level, precise amounts and full effects. Never rely on color or sound alone.

Accepted commands

  • Start or rematch: Fresh board; no startup fanfare · sound: quiet
  • Roll and land: Dice total and landing accent · sound: dice
  • Purchase a deed: Stamp the purchased space · sound: coin
  • Open bidding: Announce the auction · sound: gavel
  • Raise bid: Confirm the accepted amount · sound: bid
  • Withdraw or expire: Identify withdrawn seat or sale · sound: down
  • Develop property: Stamp the improved space · sound: build
  • Send proposal: Hand off to recipient · sound: paper
  • Accept contract: Confirm the exchange · sound: coin
  • Decline contract: Return to proposer · sound: down
  • Take guild grant: Confirm cash payment · sound: coin
  • Choose rent cover: Confirm cover · sound: bell
  • Advance turn: Ordinary transitions are quiet; final scores get a cadence · sound: quiet

Other interactions

  • Inspect / browse: Select a tile with pointer or keyboard; check focus vs landing, name, cost and owner.
  • Players / activity / rules: Switch views and dismiss sheets without losing the current decision.
  • Local setup: Check solo, pass-and-play, custom seats, map, policy and clock before replacing a game.
  • Online setup: Host, join by code, copy invite, assign seats and launch. Guests cannot change the rules.
  • Network recovery: Pause on disconnect. Rehost, rejoin, cancel or forget; historical commands must remain silent.
  • Bidding: Check presets, exact entry, insufficient cash, timer pause/expiry and final sale.
  • Rent / fee / card: Confirm the amount and card name without obstructing the next action.
  • Guild / exchange: Distinguish token location from the deed under auction; identify both guild choices.
  • Trading / building: Review both legs or project economics. Cancel or hold without a success cue.
  • Audio / motion: Sound is opt-in per browser. Muted, reduced-motion and audio-unavailable play retain text feedback.
  • Results / leave: Show winners and scores. Rematch locally or explicitly end the network table.
